About Us

Welcome to Everyday Simple Living — a blog dedicated to practical, honest advice for making everyday life a little easier.

We started this blog because we noticed something: most lifestyle advice is either wildly ambitious ("wake up at 4 a.m., journal for an hour, run ten miles") or hopelessly vague ("just be more organized"). We wanted to write the kind of articles we wished we could find — small, realistic, doable ideas that genuinely make a difference.

What we write about

  • Home & Organization — decluttering, cleaning habits, and simple systems that keep your space comfortable.
  • Money & Budgeting — beginner-friendly guides to saving, spending intentionally, and reducing financial stress.
  • Routines & Habits — morning and evening routines, and tiny habits that compound over time.
  • Food & Meal Planning — practical, budget-friendly approaches to feeding your household.

Our editorial approach

Every article on this blog is written (and regularly reviewed) with three commitments:

  • Practicality: if a tip doesn't work in real life — with limited time, money, and energy — it doesn't get published.
  • Honesty: we don't promise miracles. Small improvements, honestly described, are what you'll find here.
  • Originality: all content is written by us, in our own words, based on widely shared experience and common-sense research.

A note on accuracy

We do our best to keep content accurate and up to date, and we review and refresh articles periodically. General lifestyle and money topics can change over time, so please use the information here as helpful guidance rather than professional advice. You can read more in our Disclaimer.

Get in touch

We love hearing from readers — questions, suggestions, topic requests, or corrections. Visit our Contact page and drop us a line. If you spot something we got wrong, telling us helps every future reader too.

Thank you for reading. We hope something on this blog makes your week a little simpler.
